### Identity is persistent state

Once you name your duck, the name is stored in your browser's localStorage. It
survives reloads, restarts, even closing the tab. Every future mission can
read it — and many will.

### Why naming matters

A named thing feels different from an unnamed thing. Your duck stops being
"the kit" and becomes **someone**. That tiny psychological shift makes you
care about the missions that follow.

> If you ever want to start over, the My Pet page has a reset button.
