### Comparators are decisions

`if temperature() > 30:` is a **comparator** — a true/false test on a number.
Every if-statement, every alarm, every threshold-based decision in software
boils down to comparators.

### Why this matters

- Vaccine fridges page a technician if temp drifts
- Smoke alarms compare CO2 levels every second
- Your phone screen brightness reacts to ambient light

You're building the same logic, just smaller.
