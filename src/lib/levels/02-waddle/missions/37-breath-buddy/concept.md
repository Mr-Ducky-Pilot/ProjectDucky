### Why 4-7-8?

A doctor named Andrew Weil popularised this pattern decades ago. The math
matters less than the rhythm: a slow, steady cycle drops your heart rate and
tells your nervous system "we're safe".

It's used in trauma therapy, sleep coaching, sports recovery — and now your
duck.

### Constants

Notice the `IN`, `HOLD`, `OUT` variables at the top — those are **named
constants**. Pulling magic numbers into named variables makes code easier to
tune later. Change one number, the whole rhythm shifts.
