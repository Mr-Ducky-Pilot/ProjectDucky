### Showing feelings without words

We name our moods in lots of ways — emojis, music, the way we walk. Mood Badge
is the simplest version: pick a face, wear it.

### Why does this matter?

Naming what you feel makes it easier to handle. There's a piece of brain
research called "name it to tame it" — just saying "I'm frustrated" cuts the
intensity of frustration in half.

Your duck is now a tiny check-in. Use it once a day. See if you notice patterns.
