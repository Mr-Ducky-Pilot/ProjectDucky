### Earth is a magnet

You're standing on a giant ball of molten iron, slowly spinning. That
movement creates an invisible magnetic field that wraps around the planet.
A compass needle is just a tiny magnet that gets pulled into line with it.

The micro:bit has a **magnetometer** — a sensor that measures that pull
in three directions and works out which way is north.

### Why it sometimes lies

Other magnets confuse it. Your fridge, a metal ring, a phone, even some
desks have magnets. Try moving Ducky around the room and watch the
heading change — that's interference.

> Calibrate the compass by tilting the chip in a circle when it asks.
> You're letting it *learn* the local magnetic field.
