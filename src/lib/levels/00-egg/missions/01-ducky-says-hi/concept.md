### Why this works

Inside the micro:bit there’s a tiny grid of 25 LEDs (5 rows of 5). To make
text "scroll", the chip lights one column at a time, **really fast** — so
fast your eye blends them into a moving picture.

When you typed your name, your laptop sent each letter to the chip as a
pattern of lit and unlit dots — basically a flipbook.

### Try this next

* Type a long sentence and watch what the chip does.
* Try a single emoji symbol — does it understand?

> Computers don’t actually "know" letters. They store everything as
> patterns of on/off. Even your name.
