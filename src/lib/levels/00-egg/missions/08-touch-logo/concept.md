### How a touch sensor works

Your skin is slightly conductive — electricity passes through it. The
chip sends a tiny invisible current into the gold logo and watches what
happens. When your finger touches it, some of the current goes into *you*
and the chip notices the change.

This is called a **capacitive touch** sensor. It's the same trick your
phone screen uses (just with millions of tiny logos instead of one).

> Try it with the chip on the table vs holding it in your other hand.
> Does anything change? (Hint: yes.)
