### LEDs that *see*

This is the wild bit. The 25 little LEDs that *light up* the screen can also
*detect* light coming in. Same hardware, two jobs. The chip flips them into
"reading" mode for a tiny moment, then back to "showing" mode — too fast
for your eye to notice.

### Why this is amazing

It saves a whole separate sensor (and money, and battery, and space). When
you make stuff, this kind of trick — using one part for two jobs — is
called being **clever with what you already have**. Engineers love it.
