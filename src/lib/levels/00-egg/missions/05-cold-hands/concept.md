### A sneaky thermometer

The chip doesn't have a fancy temperature sensor — it just notices that
its **own brain runs warmer when it's warm out**. The reading is the
processor's own internal temperature, which is usually a few degrees above
the room.

### Sensors → numbers → decisions

This is the whole story of computing: take something analogue (warmth,
sound, light, motion), turn it into numbers, then make decisions with those
numbers. **All sensors do this.**

> Try cupping the chip vs blowing cold air on it. Notice how slow it is —
> there's a chunk of plastic in the way of your warm hand.
