### What's a "loop"?

The chip is doing the same thing, over and over: **show big heart →
wait → show small heart → wait → repeat**. That repeating-forever-ness is
called a *loop*, and it's how almost every game and app works.

### The two heart shapes

The big heart turns on more pixels; the small heart turns off the outer
ring. The micro:bit can swap between them faster than you can blink — that's
where the "pulse" feeling comes from.
