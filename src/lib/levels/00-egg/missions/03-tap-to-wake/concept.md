### How a button works

Inside the button is a tiny metal spring. Pressing it closes a circuit —
electricity flows. Letting go opens it. The chip notices the change and runs
your *event handler*.

> Almost everything you do on a phone — typing, scrolling, tapping a link —
> is the chip noticing a change and running a tiny handler in response.
