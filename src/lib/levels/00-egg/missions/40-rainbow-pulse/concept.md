### A wave you can see

Every dot on the LED matrix isn't just on/off — it has 10 brightness levels. By
nudging each ring of dots up and down a sine wave, you get a pulse that feels
**alive** instead of robotic.

### Why sine?

If you plot a sine wave, it looks like a calm ocean — slow at the top, slow at
the bottom, fast through the middle. That smooth shape is why this glow feels
soothing instead of stressful.

> Some apps use this exact shape to help people calm down before sleep.
> You just built one.
