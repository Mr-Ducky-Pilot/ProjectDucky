### Talking through the air

Both micro:bits have a tiny radio chip — the same kind of thing as a
wifi or Bluetooth chip, just simpler. When you press A, your chip sends
a little number through the air. Your friend's chip is listening for it
and shows the wave when it arrives.

It works up to **about 70 metres** in open air, less indoors because walls
soak up signals.

### Channels: like radio stations

If two pairs are playing in the same room, they need different *channels*
so their messages don't get mixed up — exactly like FM radio stations.

> Wifi, Bluetooth, your TV remote, garage door openers — all the same
> idea. Different signals, same trick: invisible waves through the air.
