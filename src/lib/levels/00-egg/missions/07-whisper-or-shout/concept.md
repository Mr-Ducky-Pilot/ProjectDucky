### Sound, made of numbers

Sound is just air pushing on stuff. The microphone has a tiny membrane
that gets pushed back and forth — louder push, bigger reading. The chip
samples the loudness ~100 times a second and gives you a number from 0
(silence) to 255 (very loud).

### Why a meter

The 25 LEDs aren't enough to draw a wiggly waveform, but they *are* great
for showing one number going up and down — a "VU meter" like on old
recording desks.
