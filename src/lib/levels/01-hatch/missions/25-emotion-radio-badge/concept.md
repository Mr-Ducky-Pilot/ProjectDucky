### Radio broadcasts to everyone

Both ducks are listening on the same radio channel. When yours sends, every
nearby duck hears the same message — that's a "broadcast", just like AM/FM.

Each emotion gets a number (1–6). The browser sends `R:3`, the chip transmits
3 over the air, the other chip receives 3, and the browser turns 3 back into
the matching emoji.

### What's an "encoding"?

Turning a thing (a feeling) into a small number for transmission — that's
called **encoding**. Decoding is the reverse. All of computing is
encode/decode in tiny disguises.
