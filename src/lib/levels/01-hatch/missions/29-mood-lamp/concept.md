### One feeling, three signals

Humans notice emotion through more than one sense at once — a face, a tone
of voice, body language. Ducky does the same trick in miniature: every mood
here shows up as a **face** (LED matrix), a **colour** (RGB LED), and a
**sound** (speaker), all fired together. Losing any one signal still leaves
two others carrying the same message.

### Colour mixing, for real

The RGB LED isn't picking from a preset palette — each mood is a literal
`(red, green, blue)` triple, 0–255 per channel, mixed by the LED itself.
`(255, 190, 20)` is mostly red and green with a touch of blue, which reads
as a warm gold — that's *additive* colour mixing, the same principle behind
every screen you've ever looked at.

> Try reading just the numbers before looking at the lamp — can you predict
> the colour from red/green/blue alone?
