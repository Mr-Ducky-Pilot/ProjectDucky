### What's a "threshold"?

A threshold is just a line you draw and decide: *"if the number is above
this, do A; otherwise do B."*

That tiny idea is the foundation of basically every program ever written.
Game over when health < 0. Show the unread badge when count > 0. Lock the
door when no key is detected. **All thresholds.**

### You're already programming

Picking a sensor + picking a threshold + picking what happens = a tiny
program. You just made one without writing any code.

> Try a really weird combo: temperature threshold of 12°C → Ducky stays
> sad until you take it outside. That's a real product idea.
