### A grid is a recipe for music

Read down the columns: each column is a "step in time". Read across the rows:
each row is a different sound. A lit cell means "play this sound at this step".

Most modern music is built like this. The Roland TR-808 — used in nearly every
hip-hop song ever — works the exact same way.

### Why 4×4?

Four steps = one bar of 4/4 time. Four rows = four drum sounds (kick, snare,
hi-hat, clap is the classic combo). It's the smallest grid that still lets you
write a real beat.
