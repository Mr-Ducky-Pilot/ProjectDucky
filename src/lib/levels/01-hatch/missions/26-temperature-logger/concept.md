### Data over time

Most science isn't "what's the temperature **now**". It's "how does it
**change**". A graph is the easiest way to spot change — your eye notices a
trend much faster than reading a list of numbers.

This mission grabs a temperature sample every second and plots it. Leave it
running long enough and you'll see your room's temperature breathe — radiator
turning on, sun moving, you opening a window.

### Real-world data loggers

- Vaccine fridges have these — alert if temp drifts unsafe
- Beehives have them — see when bees swarm (huge spike)
- Ocean buoys have them — track climate change

> You're using a tool real climate scientists use, just smaller.
