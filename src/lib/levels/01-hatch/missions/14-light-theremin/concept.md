### Mapping one thing onto another

Light is a number from 0 to 255. A musical note is also a number (its
frequency). The chip takes the light reading and **maps** it onto a note —
that's it. You're hearing maths.

Mapping is one of the most powerful ideas in computing: take any signal,
re-shape it, output it as something else. Speed → score. Heart-rate →
LED color. Anything → anything.

### The "scale" picker

Every note in the world doesn't sound great next to every other one. A
*scale* is a curated collection that *does* sound good together. Pick
**pentatonic** if you want everything to sound nice no matter what.

> Theremins were invented in 1920 — the first instrument played without
> touching it. Yours just uses light instead of radio waves.
