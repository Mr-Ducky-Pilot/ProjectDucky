### A frame is a moment

Animation is just a sequence of still pictures shown fast. A comic strip is the
opposite — still pictures shown **slow**, with captions filling the gaps. Both
trick the brain into completing the story.

### Why 4 panels?

Marketers call this "the four-act structure": setup, complication, climax,
resolution. Try it: your panels don't need to be amazing drawings — they just
need to be **different enough** for the reader to feel the time passing.
