### A note is a number

Music is just air vibrating at certain speeds. Faster vibrations =
higher-pitched note. **Middle C** is air vibrating about 262 times a
second. **A above middle C** is 440 times a second. The chip can buzz its
speaker at *any* of those speeds — that's how it plays a tune.

### A song is a list

Ducky records your taps as a *list* of (note, length) pairs. When you hit
play, it just walks the list one item at a time and tells the speaker
which note + for how long. That list-walking is one of the most common
patterns in code — it's called *iteration*.

> Tap a phone number, a doorbell, a movie theme. Anything works.
