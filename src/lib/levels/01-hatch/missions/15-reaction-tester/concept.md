### Reaction time, in numbers

Light from the chip travels to your eyes (about 5 nanoseconds), your brain
processes it (about 100ms), then sends a signal down your arm to your
finger (another 50ms). Most humans land between **150 and 300
milliseconds**. Pro gamers and goalkeepers train down to ~120ms.

### How the chip times you

Every chip has a tiny **clock** that ticks millions of times a second.
When the green flash starts, the chip writes down the current tick. When
you press, it writes that down too. The difference is your reaction time.

> Try it tired vs awake, with caffeine vs without, after a sprint. Same
> brain — wildly different numbers.
