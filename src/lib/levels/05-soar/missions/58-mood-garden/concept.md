### Everything you've learned, in one file

There's no new hardware here and no new wire command — Mood Garden is a
blank canvas built entirely from things earlier missions already taught:
RGB colour, built-in sounds, the LED matrix, radio, and your saved pet's
name. The point isn't to learn something new, it's to notice you already
have everything you need to build something new *yourself*.

### Why logging matters

Every mood change prints a line the board's own log convention
(`<L D name timestamp>`) — the same pattern a real science mission would use
to record data. Over a whole day, that log becomes a timeline of how a
mood-tracker actually behaves — not so different from how a real mood
journal or habit tracker works, just running on a duck instead of a phone.

> What would you want a mood journal to remember? A streak? A weekly
> average? That's exactly the kind of thing worth building on top of this.
